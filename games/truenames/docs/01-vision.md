# 01 · Vision

## Pitch
Everyone is a warlock, priest or summoner. Power comes from holding **words of power** for beings: wisps, spirits, gods. Beings dwell in the **astral**, an infinitely deep space: wisps in the shallows, gods far below, primordials perhaps only in rumor. Finding a being and grasping its words are both proof of work: real computation, done by your machine, verified by anyone.

The technical mechanic *is* the world. Hashing isn't hidden behind the fiction; it is the fiction. (On screen, though, the player only ever sees the fiction: truths, utterances, divisions, never bits or hashes.)

## Pillars
1. **Work is the magic.** Discovery = scanning cells. Learning = grinding words. Power scales with expected work spent, so it is always earned.
2. **Everything is derived.** The universe is a pure function of a public seed. Nothing is stored until a player acts, and what a player holds (signed words) verifies itself.
3. **Knowledge is the economy.** Signs (where beings dwell) can be shared and cheaply verified. Words are personal and can't be stolen, but they can be taught: anyone can grind *your* word for you, and only you can use it.
4. **Bots and humans are both first-class.** Scripts can scan and meditate. The human game is about spending that power well: timing, positioning, choice.
5. **Static evaluation.** A being's traits and a word's truths come from hashes, not from guild membership or social rules. Groups gain power through shared knowledge and labor, not special cases.
6. **One power system, many games.** The sanctum makes power; any number of games (worlds) spend it. A game can only interpret power, never create it.
7. **Prove the details, never the source.** A game learns what your words can do (element, form, might, truths) from zero-knowledge proofs, never where their beings dwell.
8. **Proofs grant, they never restrict.** Everything a player holds is information and capability. Mechanics are things you gain by proving, never taxes every game must remember to apply.

## Core loop
1. **Scry**: choose a region of the astral (element → aspect → tradition) and a depth, and scan cells for beings.
2. **Meditate**: open yourself to a being you've found (or whose sign you were shown). Words come to you for its facets, as it wills; they grow truer the longer you work, and a word is grasped once it reaches the being's bar.
3. **Bind**: choose which words to carry (four for most worlds, a deck of twelve for the Council).
4. **Cross the threshold**: each word is proven in zero knowledge, and each patron lends you a **vessel** of power for the journey.
5. **Evoke**: in the world you chose, speak your words. Each evocation draws on its vessel and **strains** your aura; spam collapses your output, and past your capacity beings answer with backlash.
6. **Grow**: every true word you hold deepens your aura's capacity, and truer words draw more and strain less.

## Two axes of power
| | A being's **might** | A word's **truths** |
|---|---|---|
| What | How vast the being is (wisp … primordial) | How truly *you* know one of its facets |
| Found by | Searching deeper (each class 16× the search) | Meditating (each truth 2× the work; each class's bar 16×) |
| Shared? | Same for everyone | Personal, bound to your aura |
| Rarity | Half as many beings each layer down | A god's first word: a season; a primordial's: centuries |

A wisp truly known, or a god barely grasped: both are real builds.
