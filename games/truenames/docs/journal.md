### M0 · Benchmark: the hash is slow
`corepack pnpm tools bench` on the dev box (8 cores, Node 22):

| op | rate / core |
|---|---|
| scry (child digest + spirit hash) | ~1,300 cells/s |
| name (poseidon4 + bits) | ~1,900 nonces/s |
| all 8 cores, full depth-6 layer scan | ~10,300 hashes/s |

poseidon-lite is ~400µs a hash. I wrote `packages/universe/src/poseidon.ts`, a straight-line version (reduced S-box, scalar state, no per-round allocation). It's **bit-identical** to poseidon-lite (enforced by a test against 200 random inputs) and only ~5% faster: BigInt mul/mod allocation is the floor. Real gains need WASM Montgomery arithmetic (est. 5–10×). **Parked as a stretch.** It doesn't change the universe, just the speed, so it can land any time.

The constants are frozen from poseidon-lite into `poseidon-constants.ts` (generated by `apps/tools/src/extract-constants.mjs`). poseidon-lite stays as a devDependency for the cross-check test.

### Freezing the universe constants
The whole-universe math that convinced me `target(d) = 4 + ⌊3d/2⌋` is fine as written:
- Expected spirits in the entire depth-d layer = `8^d / 2^target(d)` ≈ `2^(1.5d − 4)`. So depth 6 has ~32 spirits in the *whole universe*. The scan found **35**. These are the "ancient" spirits, which is great lore.
- Under a tradition prefix (depth 4), expected spirits at depth d ≈ `2^(1.5d − 16)`: 0.06 at d=8, 0.5 at d=10, 4 at d=12.
- Expected work per find is `~2 · 2^target(d)` hashes. At d=10–12 that's 1M–8M hashes, i.e. 1–9 min at ~16k/s. That lands on the "1–5 min per scry hit" target. Depth is the player's dial, so the formula is robust to future speedups.

Frozen values:
- `MIN_SPIRIT_DEPTH = 6`, `target(d) = 4 + ((3d) >> 1)`. Written with integer ops so there are no floats in existence checks.
- `MIN_NAME_BITS = 12` (docs said 8). At 8, attunement would take 0.14 s, which isn't a ritual. At 12 it's 4096 expected hashes: ~2 s on one core, a fraction of a second on all cores. It's also future-proof against WASM and faster hardware, and it lines up with `capThreshold = 12`: a freshly learned name adds no capacity, so cheap-name farming is pointless by construction.
- `SEED = 0x7472_7565_6e61_6d65_735f_7631` ("truenames_v1" in ASCII).

### M3 · Authority (headless)
- `packages/protocol`: `CastIntent`, `CastResult` (added `tag`, `strength`, `capacity`, `recoil`, `refused`), `TickResult` (pools now carry `{level, cap}`), `AuraState`, `TargetSpec`.
- `packages/authority`: `LocalAuthority implements NpcAuthority`. `Authority` gained `poolInfo(cell)` and `currentTick()` for the HUD. `NpcAuthority` adds `grantSyntheticName` / `forgetAura`, the seam for enemy shamans with fixed strengths. A server would own NPCs the same way.
- `allocate.ts`: exact weighted water-fill in one pass. Sort by limit/weight; casters who saturate below their share are served in full and the rest split proportionally. Weights are normalized to the max effective, so `2^eff` never overflows. Property tests cover Σ ≤ pool, grant ≤ min(request, cap), and "pool exhausted or everyone full".
- Backlash (v0 recoil only): chance = `clamp((strain − capacity) · 0.35, 0.25, 1)`, rolled on a seeded mulberry32. Recoil = 0.6 × grant as self-damage.
- One cast per aura per spirit per tick (dupes are refused).

**Tuning deviations from docs/03** (all in `tunables.ts`):
- `capRef = MIN_NAME_BITS`: the cast cap is `castCapBase · 2^(capExp·(eff − capRef)) · generosity`. The docs used absolute effective, which with 12-bit minimum names made caps enormous. Now a fresh name casts `castCapBase` (12) × generosity. +2 bits = ×2 cap.
- `strainDecay 0.9` (docs 0.8) and `capBase 4` (docs 3). With 0.8 and the docs' weights, the sim showed **spamming the hearth-god every tick gave the most total output**. Strain forgot too fast. With 0.9 (strain half-life ≈ 1.3 s):

  | strength 16 on hearth-god, 30 s | every tick | every 2 | every 3 | every 5 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | total output | 220 | 385 | **406** | 354 |
  | recoils | 141 | 18 | 0 | 0 |

  So spam loses output *and* hurts, and the sweet spot is ~0.6–0.8 s per cast on a light spirit. Heavier spirits want slower.
- `strainScale` tunable added (1.0 now) for quick global adjustment.
- Contention (`pnpm tools sim`): 4 casters at 12/14/18/22 share one pool at 0.1% / 13% / 33% / 54%. α = 1 is brutal at the bottom, which is by design ("the truest name drinks deepest"). **Game consequence:** enemy shaman strengths must sit close to the player's or the player gets nothing.
