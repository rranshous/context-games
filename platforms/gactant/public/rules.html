What doesn't belong here

Ludants are made by the people who play them, so what's here is what people make. Some things aren't welcome, whoever made them and however they came about:

  • Anything illegal. Above all, any sexual content involving minors: it's removed and reported to the authorities.
  • Sexual or graphic content.
  • Hate or harassment aimed at people for who they are, or at a real person.
  • Real threats, or encouraging people to hurt themselves or others.
  • Other people's private information.
  • Work that isn't yours to share, when its owner objects.

If you see something like that, press ⚑ in the game's top bar. The report goes to the people who run gactant, with a picture of the stage as it was. A publication that several people report is hidden until someone has looked at it.

Breaking these rules can get a ludant or a publication taken down.
